# 🚀 EduEarn – Project Vision

## Project Name

**EduEarn**

---

# Official Tagline

# Learn. Teach. Earn.

---

# Vision

EduEarn is an AI-powered learning marketplace where **anyone can learn** and **young students can teach and earn**.

Unlike traditional learning platforms, EduEarn creates a peer-learning ecosystem that empowers students to become educators while helping learners of all ages access affordable, high-quality education and skill development.

EduEarn believes that knowledge should not only be consumed—it should also create opportunities.

---

# Mission

To build India's most trusted AI-powered Learn → Teach → Earn ecosystem that enables millions of learners to gain knowledge while empowering young students to earn by sharing their skills.

---

# Problem Statement

Current learning platforms mainly focus on professional instructors.

Millions of talented students possess valuable academic knowledge, creative skills, and practical experience but lack a trusted platform to teach and earn.

Existing platforms generally:

* Focus on experienced instructors
* Have complex instructor onboarding
* Do not encourage peer learning
* Provide limited AI assistance
* Offer little opportunity for students to monetize their knowledge

EduEarn solves these challenges.

---

# Our Solution

EduEarn introduces a completely new ecosystem:

**Learn → Teach → Earn**

Anyone can join EduEarn to learn.

Students and young professionals can become AI-verified tutors, teach others, and earn income by sharing their knowledge.

* Senior Citizens
* Hobby Learners
* Lifelong Learners

There is **no age restriction** for learners.

---

## Tutors

During the initial launch, EduEarn will allow **students and young professionals aged 16–24** to become tutors.

Tutor Requirements:

* Complete profile
* AI Tutor Assessment
* AI Verification
* Identity verification (if required)
* Community guidelines acceptance

Once approved, tutors can:

* Create recorded courses
* Conduct live classes
* Mentor students
* Build their tutor profile
* Earn through EduEarn
