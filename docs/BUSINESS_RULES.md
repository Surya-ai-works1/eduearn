# 📋 EduEarn - Business Rules

# Purpose

This document defines the business rules that govern how EduEarn operates.

These rules ensure consistency, security, fairness, and scalability.

---

# Core Philosophy

Learn.

Teach.

Earn.

Anyone can learn.

Students and young professionals can teach.

Knowledge creates opportunities.

---

# Learner Rules

Anyone can register as a learner.

No age restriction.

Learners can:

* Browse courses
* Purchase courses
* Join live classes
* Watch recorded lessons
* Complete quizzes
* Submit assignments
* Download certificates
* Rate courses
* Review tutors
* Use AI Study Assistant

---

# Tutor Rules

Initial Launch

Tutor applications are open for students and young professionals aged 16–24.

Requirements

Complete profile

Pass AI Tutor Assessment

Identity Verification (if required)

Admin approval (if enabled)

Accepted community guidelines

---

# Tutor Responsibilities

Maintain course quality.

Respect learners.

Deliver accurate content.

Respond to student queries.

Follow platform policies.

---

# Course Rules

Each course belongs to one category.

Courses may include:

Videos

PDF Notes

Assignments

Quizzes

Resources

Certificates

Courses can be:

Draft

Pending Review

Published

Archived

Rejected

---

# Live Class Rules

Tutors schedule classes.

Students join through the platform.

Attendance is recorded.

Chat is moderated.

Recordings may be stored.

---

# Enrollment Rules

Students must enroll before accessing premium content.

Preview lessons remain public.

Certificates are issued only after meeting completion requirements.

---

# Certificate Rules

Certificates are generated automatically.

Each certificate has:

Unique ID

QR Code

Verification URL

Issue Date

Learner Name

Tutor Name

Course Name

---

# Payment Rules

Students pay through supported payment gateways.

Platform retains a commission.

Remaining amount is credited to tutor earnings.

Withdrawal requests require approval.

---

# Review Rules

Only enrolled learners can submit reviews.

One review per enrollment.

Spam or abusive reviews may be removed.

---

# AI Rules

AI assists learning.

AI never replaces tutors.

AI assessment supports tutor verification.

AI suggestions are recommendations only.

---

# Community Rules

Respect all users.

No harassment.

No hate speech.

No plagiarism.

No copyright violations.

No spam.

Violations may result in suspension.

---

# Privacy Rules

Protect user data.

Store personal information securely.

Follow applicable privacy laws.

Never share personal data without consent.

---

# Security Rules

Secure authentication.

Role-based access control.

Row Level Security.

Audit logs.

Input validation.

Secure file storage.

---

# Scalability Rules

Build every feature as reusable.

Avoid hardcoded values.

Support future expansion.

Design for millions of users.

---

# Future Expansion

Allow additional tutor categories.

Support international markets.

Introduce corporate learning.

Partner with universities.

Expand AI capabilities.

---

# Final Principle

Every product decision should support the EduEarn vision:

Empower people to learn, teach, and earn through a trusted, AI-powered learning ecosystem.
